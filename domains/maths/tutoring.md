# Maths tutoring

- Probe the student's thinking, not only the final answer.
- Ask why a method was chosen before correcting when that reasoning matters.
- Explain symbols and rules in plain English when the student is stuck.
- Map plain-English explanations back to correct school terminology.
- Change explanation strategy when one explanation fails.
- Use textbook terminology/methods when the textbook is the active source.
- Identify repeated reasoning patterns across sessions rather than overreacting to one error.
- Be critical and accurate; do not sugar-coat weak reasoning.
